Parking lots

Choose Parking lot, then click points to pull a line; stalls appear along it, spaced to fit. For a bare row with no drive aisle pick "Row of stalls" or "Parallel" (aisle 0). Use Stalls on / Flip to put them on either side; set the angle to 0° for parallel parking (Width is the depth off the line, Depth is the length along it).

Snapping

Hold Shift to turn snapping off for one move. Q / E rotates 15° (Shift = 1°, Alt = 0.1°). Arrow keys nudge 1 ft (Shift for 5 ft). While drawing, type a number and press Enter to set the next side to that exact length.

Rules

Defaults are Brecksville Codified Ordinances §1183.05 (house/townhome 2 enclosed; apartment 2.25 incl. 1 enclosed; business/office 1 per 200 sf; food & drink 1 per 50 sf). Confirm with Planning & Zoning. Offsets turn magenta when they fall short.
