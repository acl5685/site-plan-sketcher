Project parcels

Use Pick parcels to click the parcels you are developing. They merge into one boundary. Use Carve parcel or Carve shape to cut out land you are not including.

Lots

Split lots draws a line across the site; Cut out lot draws a shape that becomes a lot of its own (both are also on the toolbar). To take land out of the site altogether, use Carve shape instead. Each piece becomes its own lot: buildings are checked against their own lot's lines, and lot lines count as property lines for setbacks. With the Select tool, click inside a lot to see the largest building that fits.

shapes selected

Shift+click adds or removes a shape. Shift+drag a box selects everything fully inside it. Drag any selected shape, or use the arrow keys (Shift = 5 ft), to move them together. Q / E rotates the group (Shift = 1°, Alt = 0.1°).

Selected area

On the map: drag it to move it. Drag a square handle to move a corner, a round one to add a corner, and double-click a square one to remove it.

Selected fence

Drag the white corner handles on the map to reshape the fence.

Roads

Sidewalks: choose Sidewalk and click along a walk to draw one on its own, or select a road and set Sidewalks along it. A walk drawn through a parking area is kept clear of stalls.

Parking

Parking area: draw the road first, then choose Parking area and click the corners of the paved area. Stalls and aisles are laid out to fit the most cars; a road through it is kept clear and the aisles open onto it. A landscaping or sidewalk area drawn inside becomes an island. Select the area to change the stall size, the angle or the aisle width.
Parking row: to place one row or aisle by hand, choose Parking row, then click points to pull a line; stalls appear along it, spaced to fit. For a bare row with no drive aisle pick "Row of stalls" or "Parallel" (aisle 0). Use Stalls on / Flip to put them on either side; set the angle to 0° for parallel parking (Width is the depth off the line, Depth is the length along it).

Green space and other areas

The Green space figure at the top is the share of the site that is not built on or paved (ponds are left out). Choose Green space and click the corners to draw a landscaped area; select it to plant trees for the 3D view. Set a minimum on the Check tab to be warned when the plan falls short.

Fences

Choose Fence, then click along the fence line. Click the first corner again to close it into a loop. To fence a whole lot, choose Select, click inside the lot and use Fence this lot.

Snapping

A blue mark shows where the point will land and the line it is on. With an angle locked, the point slides along that angle onto the line. Hold Shift to place a point freely.
Hold Shift to turn snapping off for one move. Q / E rotates 15° (Shift = 1°, Alt = 0.1°). Arrow keys nudge 1 ft (Shift for 5 ft). While drawing, type a number and press Enter to set the next side to that exact length.

Rules

Defaults are Brecksville Codified Ordinances §1183.05 (house/townhome 2 enclosed; apartment 2.25 incl. 1 enclosed; business/office 1 per 200 sf; food & drink 1 per 50 sf). Confirm with Planning & Zoning. Offsets turn magenta when they fall short.

3D models

Built-in trees and a car are always available. To use your own, export from Blender with File › Export › glTF 2.0, Format: glTF Binary (.glb), then add the file here or drop it anywhere on the page. Pick them on a landscaping area (trees), a parking area or row (cars) or a building (replaces the generated building, fitted to its footprint). Models stay in this browser; a project only remembers their names.
